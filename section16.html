Owlet – Photo Art Direction


Creative Brief Development · Shot List · On-Set Art Direction · Photo Selects


I shaped Owlet's brand photography direction to focus on intimate moments between parents and baby in warm, natural home environments. Capturing everyday life rather than performing it: genuine smiles, quiet moments, and small rituals. Light is warm and natural, settings feel lived-in, and Owlet products are present without dominating the frame.

The result is a growing evergreen brand library that spans the core product line, Dream Sock, Dream Duo 3, and Dream Sight, and positions Owlet not as a piece of technology but as trusted, reassuring technology that intergrates seamlessly into everyday routines.

The following are selects from multiple shoots I planned end-to-end: developing creative briefs, sourcing and collaborating with external creative partners, building shot lists, and providing on-site art direction.
