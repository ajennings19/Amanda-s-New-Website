Owlet – Photo Art Direction


Creative Direction
Visual Strategy
Shot List Development
On-Set Art Direction



Owlet is a baby monitor brand built around the peace of mind that comes with knowing baby is healthy and safe. The photography needed to catch up to that emotional truth. I led a shift in photography away from staged product imagery toward moments that felt warmer, more intimate, and genuinely human — directing photographers and producers, building shot lists around brand and campaign needs, and carrying the new aesthetic through across every asset.
